Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We're Here to Help.

Find answers to some common questions about our pharmacy and home care services.

Do I need a prescription for all medicines?

Some medicines require a valid prescription while others are available without one. Our qualified pharmacy team can guide you appropriately.

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes. We offer convenient delivery of medicines and selected healthcare products. Contact us to confirm availability and delivery details.

Do you provide vaccination services?

Vaccination services may be available depending on the vaccine and current service availability. Contact us before visiting.

Can I request a medication refill?

Yes. You can contact our pharmacy team to ask about your regular medication refill and any prescription requirements.

Do you provide home nursing care?

We provide home-based care support, including services such as wound care, elderly care, medication support and post-hospital recovery.
